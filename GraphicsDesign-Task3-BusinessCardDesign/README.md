# Business Card Design

## Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Oasis Infobyte Graphics Design Internship** under the **Graphics Design Track (Task 3).**

The objective of this project was to design a professional double-sided business card for a fictional company while maintaining a clean layout, strong typography, and consistent branding. The business card was created for **FutureTech Summit 2026**, featuring a modern and professional design that effectively presents personal and company information.

The design follows standard business card dimensions and demonstrates the use of whitespace, visual hierarchy, and branding consistency. The front side highlights the company logo, name, and designation, while the back side provides complete contact information in an organized and easy-to-read format.

---

## Features

- Professional double-sided business card design
- Front side includes:
  - Company logo
  - Full name
  - Job title
- Back side includes:
  - Phone number
  - Email address
  - Website
  - Business location
- Clean and modern layout
- Consistent color palette and branding
- Proper typography with clear visual hierarchy
- Effective use of whitespace for readability
- Designed using standard business card dimensions
- High-resolution export suitable for print and digital use
